# Minor Catastrophe Bot — Terms of Service

**Effective Date:** November 19, 2026

## 1. Purpose
Minor Catastrophe Bot is a privately operated Discord bot designed for the Minor Catastrophe World of Warcraft guild. It provides guild activity coordination, dungeon planning, missions, bounties, trivia, and community participation features.

## 2. Authorized Use
The bot is intended exclusively for the Minor Catastrophe Discord server. Members must comply with Discord's Terms of Service and the guild's community rules.

## 3. Availability
The bot is provided free of charge and on an "as available" basis. Features may be modified, suspended, or discontinued without prior notice.

## 4. Member Conduct
Members may not abuse bot commands, intentionally disrupt guild activities, exploit reward systems, or use the bot to harass others.

## 5. Data and Privacy
Use of the bot is subject to the Minor Catastrophe Bot Privacy Policy.

## 6. Administration
Guild administrators may moderate bot activity, correct records, or restrict access to protect the community.

## 7. Changes
These Terms may be updated as the bot evolves. Material changes will be communicated through the guild's Discord server.

## 8. Contact
For questions about these Terms, contact the Minor Catastrophe server administrators through Discord.
