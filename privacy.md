# Minor Catastrophe Bot — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 19, 2026

## 1. Overview
Minor Catastrophe Bot is a privately hosted Discord application used exclusively within the Minor Catastrophe World of Warcraft guild.

## 2. Information Collected
Depending on which features members use, the bot may process and store:

- Discord user IDs and server IDs
- Discord display names when needed for guild activities
- Dungeon and event registrations
- Guild missions, bounties, and participation records
- Trivia results, points, and achievements
- User-submitted content associated with bot commands

The bot is not intended to collect passwords, payment information, or private direct messages.

## 3. How Information Is Used
Information is used to operate guild activities, track participation, manage events, display results, and support administrative functions.

## 4. Data Storage
The bot operates on a privately managed Windows computer. Persistent activity records are stored locally using SQLite.

## 5. Information Sharing
Bot records are not sold to advertisers or used for commercial marketing. Some participation information may be visible to other guild members through bot commands and announcements.

## 6. Data Retention
Records may be retained while they are needed for guild activities or historical tracking. Guild administrators may remove records when they are no longer necessary.

## 7. Data Deletion Requests
Members may contact the Minor Catastrophe server administrators through Discord to request access to or deletion of their bot-associated information. Requests will be reviewed and handled within a reasonable timeframe, subject to legitimate operational requirements.

## 8. Security
Reasonable measures are taken to restrict access to the bot's hosting environment and stored information. No storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

## 9. Changes
This policy may be updated when the bot's features or data practices change. Material changes will be communicated through the guild's Discord server.

## 10. Contact
For privacy questions or data requests, contact the Minor Catastrophe server administrators through Discord.
